About Me

Hi there! I'm a 1st year EECS PhD student at UC Berkeley advised by Professor Preeya Khanna. My research interests are closed-loop BCI, sensorimotor neuroscience, and robotics.

Previously, I completed my Masters and Bachelor's also at Berkeley. I worked on computational neuroscience at Knight Lab, Multimodal BCI at Fluid Interfaces, and ML for Healthcare at C.H.E.N.lab.

Some of my non-academic interests are basketball, climbing, video games, spending time with my cats, cooking, reading, and traveling.
